What is inside

Six things, all of them for you

Nothing here is filler. Each one is something I wanted us to have and could not find in a single place anywhere else.

The days that matter

Our start date, your birthday, and any other day we decide is worth counting. The home screen opens on the numbers instead of a timeline of nothing.

Notes that wait for you

Leave a note with a mood attached and read it whenever you like. The ones that land get hearts back. It is the good part of a text thread, without the noise.

Moments, not a photo dump

The small things go in as they happen, into their own quiet gallery, instead of vanishing into a camera roll neither of us ever opens again.

Cycle and care, handled with respect

Cycle, mood, symptoms and the care log, built for you to actually use and for me to simply be aware of. Never clinical, never a lecture.

Chat that arrives instantly

One live stream carries the whole room. Messages, typing and presence all ride the same connection, so it feels immediate instead of refreshed.

Calls, the real kind

Voice and video running straight between our two phones. No dialling into a platform, no lobby, no account to hand over first.

Care, done properly

You write it down. I just learn it.

The care section knows whose phone it is on. You get every control there is. I get the picture, and exactly one way to help.

Yours, all of it

Mood, flow, symptoms, period start and the care log are yours to write. I cannot edit a single line.

Mine to read

I can see the log and the cycle. Nothing is hidden from me, and nothing is mine to change either.

The one pen I get

I can write you a note, and it lands in your feed signed from me. That is the whole of it, on purpose.

Live sync

Two phones, one room, nothing to refresh

Everything travels over one live stream the whole app shares. Type something and it is already there. Start a call and your phone rings where it sits.

Private by design

What happens in here stays in here

Just the two of us

The whole app sits inside one room with exactly two members. No discovery, no suggested people, no way for anyone else to wander in.

Nothing is sold

No analytics pipeline, no ad networks, no third party reading our notes to work out what to sell us next. There is no tracking here to switch off.

Ours to delete

Everything can be cleared from the app. When something is deleted it is gone from the room, not quietly archived on a server we cannot see.
