What is inside

Everything the two of you keep track of, in one place

Six things that actually get used, built around how the two of you already live, not around an engagement metric.

Countdowns that matter

Her birthday, your anniversary, and any day you decide is worth counting. The home screen leads with the numbers, not a timeline of nothing.

Notes that land softly

Leave a note with a mood attached. She can read it whenever, and the ones that hit get hearts back. It is the good part of a text thread with none of the noise.

Moments, not a photo dump

Drop the small things in as they happen. Photos get their own quiet gallery instead of disappearing into a camera roll you never open again.

Cycle and care, handled with respect

Cycle tracking, mood, symptoms and the care log, all built for one person to actually use and the other to simply be aware of. Never clinical, never a lecture.

Chat that arrives instantly

One live stream carries the room. Messages, typing, presence and delivery all ride the same connection, so it feels immediate instead of refreshed.

Calls, the real kind

Voice and video calling that runs directly between your two devices. No dialling a platform, no joining a lobby, no account to hand over first.

Care, done properly

One person logs it. The other just knows.

The care section reads the room. It greets her as you, and she gets the full set of controls. He gets the picture, plus exactly one way to contribute.

Esther: full logging

Mood, flow, symptoms, period start and the care log, all hers to write.

Israel: view only

He reads the log and the cycle. Nothing is editable from his side, and nothing is hidden from him either.

And one thing he can write

Write a note. It lands in her feed signed from him. That is the only pen he gets, on purpose.

Live sync

Two phones, one room, no refresh button

Every change travels over a single live stream that the whole app shares. Type a message and it is there. Start a call and the other phone rings.

Messages, typing and presence on one connection
Voice and video call straight between devices
Keeps working if the signal drops, then catches up
Push alerts when the app is closed
Private by design

Your relationship is not a data source

Two people in the room

The whole app lives inside one private room with exactly two members. There is no discovery, no suggested people, and no way to stumble into it.

Nothing is sold

No analytics pipeline, no ad networks, no third party reading your notes to work out what to sell you next. The app has no tracking to switch off.

Yours to delete

Everything can be cleared from the app. When you delete something it is gone from the room, not quietly archived on a server you cannot see.
